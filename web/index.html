Replay assumptions

Both forecasts use earlier sales. Opening stock uses the preceding 28-day average. Each method gets one delivery before sales on its arrival day. The simulation uses recorded sales as demand and treats any shortage as a missed sale.

The shared limit is the smaller unrestricted order. Each method allocates its next pack where it expects the pack to cover the most sales. Earlier projected shortages break ties.
